# SecureBank Security Practices

We protect your money and your data with multiple layers of defence.

## Encryption

- All data in transit between your browser or mobile app and our servers is encrypted with TLS 1.3.
- All data at rest in our databases is encrypted with AES-256.
- Stored card numbers and account numbers are tokenised — even our internal systems do not see the raw values.

## Authentication

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is supported via SMS one-time codes and authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password).
- MFA is mandatory for any transfer over $5,000, any change to your registered contact details, and any sensitive support request.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and we check them against known-breach lists at every login.

## Fraud monitoring

- Every transaction passes through a real-time fraud-detection model that scores the risk based on amount, recipient, location, and historical patterns.
- High-risk transactions trigger an instant push notification or SMS asking you to confirm before they post.
- If you do not confirm a flagged transaction within 30 minutes, it is automatically declined and the funds are returned.

## Liability

- You are NOT liable for any unauthorized transaction reported within 60 days of your statement date (Regulation E).
- Lost or stolen debit cards: report within 2 business days and your liability is capped at $50.
- Credit card unauthorized charges: $0 liability with timely reporting.

## How to report fraud

- Phone — 1-800-FRAUD-LINE, available 24/7.
- In the mobile app — Settings → Security → Report Fraud.
- Email — fraud@securebank.example.com (do NOT email account or card numbers; we will reply with a secure portal link).
