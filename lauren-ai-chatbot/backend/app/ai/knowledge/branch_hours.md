# SecureBank Branch and Support Hours

## Branch hours (all locations, US Pacific Time)

- Monday through Friday — 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
- Saturday — 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM
- Sunday — closed

## Holiday closures

Branches are closed on the following federal holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

On Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve branches close early at 1:00 PM.

## Phone support

- Customer service — 1-800-SECURE-BANK (1-800-732-8732), available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Lost or stolen card — 1-800-LOST-CARD, available 24/7.
- Fraud reporting — 1-800-FRAUD-LINE, available 24/7.

## Online and mobile

- Online banking — available 24/7 at securebank.example.com (subject to occasional maintenance windows announced in-app).
- Mobile app — iOS and Android, available 24/7.
- Live chat — Monday through Friday 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Pacific Time.
