# SecureBank Fee Schedule

All fees in US dollars. Last updated 2026.

## Account maintenance

- Standard Checking — $0 monthly maintenance.
- Premium Checking — $5 per month, waived with $500 minimum daily balance.
- Regular Savings — $0 monthly maintenance.
- High-Yield Savings — $0 monthly maintenance with $10,000 average balance; otherwise $10 per month.
- Money Market — $0 with $5,000 minimum daily balance; otherwise $12 per month.

## Transaction fees

- ATM withdrawal at SecureBank ATM — free.
- ATM withdrawal at out-of-network ATM — $2.50 per withdrawal (we do not reimburse other-bank surcharges).
- Domestic wire transfer — $15 outbound, free inbound.
- International wire transfer — $40 outbound, $15 inbound.
- ACH transfer — free for both inbound and outbound.

## Overdraft

- Overdraft fee — $35 per item, capped at three per business day.
- Overdraft protection transfer (linked savings) — $10 per transfer.
- Returned item fee — $35.

## Other

- Stop payment — $30 per request.
- Account closure within 90 days of opening — $25.
- Inactive account — $2.50 per month after 12 months of no activity.
- Replacement debit card — $5 standard delivery, $25 expedited.
- Statement copy older than 12 months — $5 per statement.
