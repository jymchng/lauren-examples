# How to Open a SecureBank Account

You can open a Checking, Savings, Money Market, or CD account online or in person at any branch.

## Online opening

1. Visit securebank.example.com and click "Open an account".
2. Choose the account type (Checking, Savings, Money Market, or CD).
3. Enter your Social Security Number, date of birth, and government-issued photo ID details.
4. Verify your address (a recent utility bill or lease is acceptable).
5. Fund the account with a minimum $25 opening deposit (Checking and Savings) or $1,000 for a CD.
6. Most applications are approved within 5 minutes for credit-qualified customers.

## In-person opening

Visit any branch during open hours with the following documents:

- Government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID).
- Social Security card or ITIN documentation.
- Proof of address (utility bill, lease, or recent bank statement dated within 60 days).
- Initial deposit — $25 for Checking or Savings, $1,000 for a CD, $2,500 for a Money Market account.

A personal banker will walk you through the application and the account is usually open the same day.

## For students

Students aged 16 to 22 can open Student Checking with no monthly fee, no overdraft penalties, and no minimum balance. A parent or guardian co-signature is required for applicants under 18.

## For non-citizens

Permanent residents and visa holders may open accounts with proof of legal residency in addition to the standard documents listed above. We accept ITINs in lieu of SSNs.
