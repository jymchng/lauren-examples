# SecureBank Product Catalog

SecureBank offers four account families: Checking, Savings, Money Market, and Certificates of Deposit (CDs).

## Checking accounts

- **Standard Checking** — no monthly fee. Free debit card. Online bill pay. Mobile check deposit. APY 0.01%.
- **Premium Checking** — $5 monthly fee, waived with $500 minimum daily balance. Free wires (one per month). APY 0.10%.
- **Student Checking** — for customers aged 16-22. No monthly fee. No overdraft fees. APY 0.01%.

## Savings accounts

- **Regular Savings** — APY 2.50%. Six free withdrawals per month; $5 fee per excess withdrawal.
- **High-Yield Savings** — APY 4.75%. Requires $10,000 minimum opening deposit. Six free withdrawals per month.

## Money Market account

- **Money Market** — APY 4.20%. Tiered rates above $25,000 balance. Includes a debit card and check writing.

## Certificates of Deposit (CDs)

- **3-month CD** — APY 4.40%. Minimum $1,000.
- **6-month CD** — APY 4.65%. Minimum $1,000.
- **12-month CD** — APY 4.90%. Minimum $1,000.
- **24-month CD** — APY 5.10%. Minimum $1,000.

Early withdrawal from a CD incurs a 90-day interest penalty.
